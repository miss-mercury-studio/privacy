Privacy Policy

Cover Player and Cover Queue by Miss Mercury Studio · Effective 31 October 2026

In short: the apps do not collect, share or sell personal data. They read what YouTube Music is playing on your phone, only to show it on the widget. They have no accounts, ads or analytics. The only thing that leaves your phone is a request for each queue thumbnail image.

Who this covers

This policy covers two Android apps from the same developer, Miss Mercury Studio: Cover Player and Cover Queue. Both are widgets for the cover screen of Samsung Galaxy Z Flip phones that show and control what YouTube Music is playing. They are not official YouTube or Google products and are not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

What the apps read

The apps read the playback information that YouTube Music publishes on your phone: the track title, artist, album, cover art, the play queue, the playback position, and whether the track is liked. This is used only to draw the widget and to send your taps (play, pause, skip, jump to a track, like) back to YouTube Music.

Notification access

Android only lets an app see and control another app's playback if it has been given notification access. That is the only reason the apps ask for it. They do not read, store or transmit the content of your notifications, and they ignore every app except YouTube Music. You grant the access yourself, from the widget's setup message, and you can take it back at any time in Settings › Notifications › Notification access. Without it the widget only shows a message asking for it.

What is stored on your phone

  • The titles, artists and small thumbnails of the last 20 tracks you listened to, so the queue can show what played before the current track.
  • In Cover Player, which page of the widget (now playing or queue) you last chose.

This is kept in the apps' private storage on your phone. The developer cannot see it. Clearing an app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, removes it. Tracks you mark to be skipped are kept in memory only and are forgotten when the playback session ends.

What leaves your phone

To show thumbnails in the queue, the apps download each thumbnail image from the web address that YouTube Music supplies for it, which points to Google's image servers. Like any request on the internet, this lets the server see your IP address and basic device and connection details. The requests contain no account, track history or other personal information from the apps. This is the only network use, and it is the reason for the apps' internet permission.

The images are served by Google, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The apps do not send anything to the developer or to any other third party.

Backups

Android's backup feature may copy an app's stored data, here the short track list and the page choice, to your own Google account backup, depending on your phone's settings. That is managed by Android and Google, not by the apps or the developer, and you can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

No accounts, ads, analytics or sharing

The apps have no sign-in and no accounts, show no ads, include no analytics or crash-reporting services, and do not share, sell or use your information for advertising or any other purpose.

Data retention

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to retain on any server. Data on your phone stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, as described above. Thumbnail images are requested from Google and are subject to Google's own retention.

Security

Because the apps keep their data in private storage on your phone and send nothing to the developer, there is no developer-side database to protect. Android's app sandbox protects the stored data. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your rights

The developer collects and holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on the developer's side. You control everything the apps keep on your phone through the choices below. If you have a question about your privacy, contact us at the address below.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Turn off notification access to stop the apps from seeing playback information.
  • Clear the app's storage, or uninstall it, to remove the stored track list.
  • Remove the widget from the cover screen at any time.
